# SnapSpace — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 4 Sept 2026**


## The short version

SnapSpace is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no
servers of its own, and no analytics. Your progress lives on your device and
nowhere else.

The one exception is advertising. SnapSpace shows adverts supplied by Google
AdMob, and Google collects data through those adverts. That is the only part of
this policy that is about anyone but you and your phone.

## What SnapSpace stores on your device

Three small files, written by the game and readable only by it:

- **Progress** — which levels you have completed.
- **Preferences** — whether sound is muted.
- **Entitlements** — which hints you have unlocked.

These stay on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere, they are not backed
up to us, and we cannot see them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Advertising

SnapSpace shows adverts from **Google AdMob** so that the game can be free.
There are two kinds:

- **Interstitial adverts**, shown occasionally between levels.
- **Rewarded video adverts**, shown only when you choose to watch one in
  exchange for a hint.

To serve those adverts, Google may collect and process:

- your device's **advertising identifier**
- **device information** — model, operating system version, language, and
  approximate location derived from your IP address
- **IP address**
- **information about the adverts you were shown** and whether you interacted
  with them

This data is collected by Google, not by us. We never see it, and we receive
only aggregate reports of how many adverts were shown.

Google's use of this data is governed by their own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services —
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Advertising — https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Your choices about advertising

**In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland**, and in US states with comparable laws,
SnapSpace asks for your consent before any personalised advertising happens,
using Google's User Messaging Platform. The consent form appears the first time
you open the game. You can change your answer at any time: open the level menu
and tap **Ad Settings**.

If you decline, or if you never answer, SnapSpace still shows adverts — but
non-personalised ones, which are chosen from the content of the app rather than
from anything about you.

**On iOS**, SnapSpace also asks Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission
before anything else. Declining it prevents the advertising identifier being
used to track you across apps, and the game works exactly the same either way.

**On any device**, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your
system settings:

- Android — Settings → Google → Ads
- iOS — Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

## Children

SnapSpace is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children under 13,
we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and it
is not marketed to them.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app,
contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

## Legal bases (EEA/UK)

- **Consent** — personalised advertising, where you have given it.
- **Legitimate interests** — showing non-personalised advertising in order to
  fund a free app, and keeping the app working correctly.

You may withdraw consent at any time through **Ad Settings** in the level menu.
Withdrawing it does not remove adverts; it makes them non-personalised.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete,
or object to the processing of personal data about you, and to lodge a complaint
with your data protection authority.

Because SnapSpace holds nothing about you on any server, requests about data
collected through adverts are best directed to Google using the links above. For
anything else, contact us.

## Data retention

We retain nothing, because we collect nothing. The files on your device last
until you delete the app. Data collected by Google through adverts is retained
according to Google's own policy.
